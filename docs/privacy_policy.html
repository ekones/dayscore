Privacy Policy for DayScore

DayScore is provided as a free web application for users who want to understand the real cost of their spending in terms of time worked. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information and what data, if any, is collected during usage.

DayScore does not display advertisements and does not use advertising-related tracking technologies.

Information Collection and Use

The DayScore app does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. No account creation, login, or personal data input is required to use the app.

All data, including income values, expense entries, and calculated statistics, is stored locally in the browser using local storage on your device. This data remains under your control and is not sent to a server by the application.

Local Storage

DayScore uses browser local storage to retain your entries so the app can continue where you left off. This data is stored only in your browser and is not used for marketing, analytics, or profiling.

Cookies

The DayScore app does not use cookies or any cookie-based tracking technologies.

Permissions

DayScore may require limited browser permissions for normal operation, such as local data storage, but no personal or sensitive data is requested or collected.

Children’s Privacy

DayScore does not collect personal data from children under the age of 13, nor from any users. The app is designed to work without requiring personal information at any stage.

Third-Party Services

DayScore does not integrate any third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, or user tracking. No external service has access to user data through the app.

Security

Since DayScore does not collect or store personal information on a remote server, there is no central database of user data to expose. The app still follows standard best practices to keep local browser data secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page and will become effective as soon as they are available.

Contact

If you have questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact the developer.
